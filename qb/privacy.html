Privacy Policy

Pivot DJ Automation App
Effective date: October 29, 2026

Pivot DJ LLC (“Pivot DJ,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates an internal business automation application (“App”) used to support our business operations, including integrations with QuickBooks Online. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information through the App.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the App's configuration and authorized integrations, we may access or process:

  • Accounting information: invoices, payments, transaction records, customer and vendor details, and related QuickBooks data.
  • Business information: event details, booking records, contact information, and operational data.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens, connection status, application logs, error reports, and information necessary to operate and troubleshoot automations.

The specific information accessed depends on the permissions granted to the App and the services connected to it.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate and maintain business automations.
  • Synchronize and process business and accounting records.
  • Monitor failures, troubleshoot problems, and maintain security.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money.

3. QuickBooks and Third-Party Services

When you authorize a QuickBooks connection, the App accesses information permitted by the authorization granted to it. Information may be transmitted to or received from Intuit and other services necessary to perform the requested functions.

Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Our use of QuickBooks data is subject to applicable Intuit developer requirements and permissions.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not disclose information except as reasonably necessary to operate the App, provide connected services, protect our rights and systems, comply with legal obligations, or respond to valid legal requests.

We may use service providers for hosting, infrastructure, monitoring, or related technical functions. Such providers may process information on our behalf.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, security, and legal or accounting obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may revoke the App's access to your QuickBooks account through the applicable Intuit account or authorization settings. Revoking access may prevent automations from functioning.

Revoking access does not necessarily delete information already stored in the App, connected services, backups, or legally required business records. To request access to or deletion of information held by Pivot DJ, contact us using the contact information published on our website.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or our practices change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date.
