Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you DJ? +
We play weddings, corporate events, private parties, and school dances. Each event is unique, so we work closely with you to create the perfect atmosphere.
What is your pricing? +
Pricing varies based on a lot of factors!

Our wedding packages start at $1000, and event packages start at $400.

If you want to get some more specific numbers, reach out & we'll send you a quote!
How far in advance should I book? +
For weddings and major events, we recommend booking 3-6 months in advance. However, we may have availability at any time, so it's always worth asking!
Can I provide a playlist? +
Absolutely! We would love to hear your music preferences and must-play songs.
What's the process for booking? +
1. We'll chat about your event, and send you a quote!

2. If the quote looks good, we'll send you a contract & require a 50% deposit to secure your date.

3. We'll chat again to go over the details & make a playlist based on your preferences.

4. We'll show up early to set up, and make sure everything goes smoothly!
Do you provide extras, like venue uplighting or a photo booth? +
No. We provide a simple wedding DJ service, at a more affordable price point than most DJs in the Triangle and Triad areas!
